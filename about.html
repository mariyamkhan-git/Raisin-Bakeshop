About RAISIN

The Story of Mariyam

Mariyam Raisin's passion for baking began in her grandmother's kitchen, where generations of family recipes were guarded like treasure. What started as a hobby evolved into a mission: to bring luxury and elegance to every bite.

RAISIN Bakeshop was founded with a singular belief: that desserts should be more than sweet—they should be experiences. Each creation is a blend of tradition, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Nestled in the heart of our town, Raisin Bakeshop began as a humble dream — to bring the warmth of home-baked treats to every table. Inspired by her mother, Mariyam inherited not just recipes, but a true craft.

At Raisin, we bake the old-fashioned way — with hands, heart, and heritage. Every item is made fresh using locally sourced ingredients and time-tested techniques.

Why "Raisin"? Because it represents patience, care, and transformation — just like our baking process.

Whether you're here for a sweet treat or a special occasion, you're not just a customer — you're part of the Raisin family.
